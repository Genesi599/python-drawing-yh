# Flat eukaryotic cell template attribution

This template is a composite. Keep this file with redistributed or published
copies and check each upstream license for the intended use.

## Base cell

- Nucleus and rough-ER image: adapted from Figure 4.8, OpenStax Biology 2e,
  section 4.3 Eukaryotic Cells. OpenStax Biology 2e is distributed under
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The local adaptation removes labels and surrounding
  organelles and harmonizes the crop with the template.
  Source: https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/4-3-eukaryotic-cells
- Golgi apparatus: adapted from Disrupted Golgi, NIH BioArt 627, NIAID Visual
  & Medical Arts; public domain.
  Source: https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/627
- Mitochondrion, vacuole, and secreted-protein icons: Servier Medical Art via
  Bioicons; CC BY 3.0. Geometry and colors are adapted for this template.
  Source: https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons
- Lipid bilayer, ECM curves, transmembrane proteins, cell projection, and
  apposed-cell junction: project-created editable SVG geometry.
